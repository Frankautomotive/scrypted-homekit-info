Privacy policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026.

Information accessed

With the account owner’s authorization, the integration accesses the selected cameras’ names, identifiers, room information, status, live video and audio, and supported motion and person events. Google authorization is used solely to operate the owner’s camera integration.

Purpose and data flow

This information is used to display cameras in Apple Home, relay activity events, and support recording when enabled by the owner. Google and Nest provide the camera services. Google Cloud Pub/Sub and Scrypted Cloud deliver event messages. The Scrypted server and Apple Home process video as required for viewing and recording; enabled HomeKit Secure Video recordings are stored through Apple iCloud.

Access, storage, and retention

Authorization credentials and tokens are stored in the owner’s Scrypted installation. Access is intended for the operator and the household members they authorize. Video recordings, temporary buffers, event queues, and operational logs follow the relevant service settings and the operator’s configuration. This informational website does not receive camera footage, device events, or authorization credentials.

Sharing and limited use

The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Data is used only for the camera functions described here and processed by the services needed to provide those functions. Those services also apply their own privacy policies. The operator’s use of data received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke the integration’s Google authorization, remove stored credentials from Scrypted, remove cameras from Apple Home, and manage or delete recordings through the relevant service. Removing this public website alone does not revoke camera access or delete recordings.

This website

These are static informational pages with no analytics, advertising scripts, forms, or application sign-in. GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under its own privacy policy.

Contact and updates

For questions, contact the operator through the project’s public issue tracker. Do not post credentials, camera footage, or other sensitive information in public issues. This policy should be updated when the integration’s data practices change.
