Scope

Scrypted Nest HomeKit is a private camera integration operated for the account owner and their authorized household. This website provides information about that integration; it does not provide public registration, camera streams, or access to a home.

Use and authorization

The operator manages the Google authorization, camera access, server, household permissions, and recording settings. Use of the integration must comply with the terms applicable to Google, Nest, Scrypted, GitHub, and Apple services.

Availability

Features depend on the cameras, network, self-managed server, and third-party services. Availability, event delivery, and recordings are not guaranteed. The integration is not an emergency-response service.

Independent project

This personal integration is not an official product of or endorsed by Google, Nest, Apple, or Scrypted. Their names identify the services and software used.

The privacy policy describes data use. Questions can be directed to the operator through the project’s public issue tracker.
