Personal camera integration

Scrypted Nest HomeKit

OverviewPrivacyTerms

A private integration for viewing authorized Nest cameras in Apple Home and recording selected activity with HomeKit Secure Video.

How it works

The account owner authorizes selected cameras through Google. A self-managed Scrypted server connects those cameras and their activity events to Apple Home. Live viewing and recording depend on the owner’s permissions, camera settings, and Apple Home configuration.

Personal use

This integration is operated for the account owner and their authorized household. It is not offered as a public camera service, and this website has no account registration or camera access.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use for details.
